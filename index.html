Space Adventure

Cosmic Storm · arcade

One thumb. One endless asteroid storm.

Steer a neon ship through a storm that keeps getting faster. Grab shields and slow motion, chase your best score and unlock 16 ships, from Common to Legendary.

  • 16 ships
  • Missions and streaks
  • Game Center

Echo Climb

Fall to Rise · puzzle platformer

Every fall leaves a stone you can stand on.

A little moon-white runner dashes on its own; you only tap to jump. Fall into lava and it cools into a bridge, freeze in a laser and your echo blocks the beam. The way up is built from your own falls.

  • 20 handmade levels
  • One tap
  • Moonlit and calm

Pour & Freeze

Ice Puzzle · physics

Tap to pour. Tap to freeze. Fill every glass.

Real liquid that splashes, pools and spills. Freeze the stream right where it is: ice plugs a leak, raises a dam and puts a lid on a full glass so the next colour can pour over it.

  • Real flowing liquid
  • Three stars a level
  • Bright and gentle
Coming soon to the App Store Privacy

Ricochet Pop

Bank shots · comic puzzle

Bank the shot. Pop the picture.

A pixel picture made of coloured blocks hangs over your cannon. Every shot pops its own colour and bounces off everything else, so the best shots go round the corner. Clear the picture before the shots run out.

  • Pixel pictures
  • Trick shots
  • KA-POW!
In development

Domino Bells

Chain reaction · music puzzle

Set the pieces. Ring the tune.

Place dominoes, arrows and splitters, press GO and watch the chain run. Every bell must ring, in the order of the tune. Get the timing right and the board plays a melody.

  • 20 levels
  • A tune in every level
  • Warm wooden table
